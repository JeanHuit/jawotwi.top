Hiatus Over

Contents

  1. What Will Change?
  2. What Won’t Change?

After an unplanned and extended break, this blog is finally coming back to life.

Hopefully, it will stay active this time around.

A lot has happened during this silent stretch, and I look forward to sharing new insights along the way. The last post here was back in 2021. I’ve heard that every blogger hits a hiatus eventually—mine just happened to be a long one.

Between a job change, a new addition to the family, and the ongoing process of adjusting, four years slipped by. That said, while there may still be occasional lulls, I expect to post far more regularly—definitely not with another four-year gap!

What Will Change?

Not much, but here’s what I plan to focus on:

Refreshing the website

Narrowing down to a few key topics and exploring them in more depth

Embracing long-form writing

What Won’t Change?

Hard to say. Time will tell.
