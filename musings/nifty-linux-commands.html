*

Netstat -------

Short for network status, this command when run without options netstat , can get carried away. Displaying all incoming and outgoing network requests at a go. This can be daunting and one may easily get lost trying to sift through all that data. Piping | the data through less will make it easier for you to peruse and understand where your data is going, from what application and from what point data is being returned. eg.

netstat -nr | less

Using the - nr option, gives you access to your routing table in number format, i.e shows you the network address.
